Entity-Relationship (ER) Architecture

Identified Entities:
  • Patient: patient_id (PK), name, email (UQ), date_of_birth, gender, phone, address
  • Doctor: doctor_id (PK), name, email (UQ), phone, specialization, experience_years, department_id (FK)
  • Department: department_id (PK), department_name (UQ), location, floor
  • Appointment: appointment_id (PK), patient_id (FK), doctor_id (FK), date, start_time, end_time, status, consultation_type
  • MedicalHistory: history_id (PK), patient_id (FK), doctor_id (FK), record_date, conditions, diagnosis, treatment, notes
  • Schedule: schedule_id (PK), doctor_id (FK), day_of_week, start_time, end_time, break_start, break_end
  • Medication: medication_id (PK), medicine_name (UQ), type, unit, price, stock_quantity
  • Prescription: prescription_id (PK), appointment_id (FK), doctor_id (FK), medication_id (FK), dosage, frequency, duration, instructions, quantity
  • Billing: bill_id (PK), patient_id (FK), appointment_id (FK), bill_date, total_amount, payment_status, payment_mode
Cardinality & Relationships:
  • Patient Books Appointment: 1 : N (One patient can book multiple appointments)
  • Doctor Assigned To Appointment: 1 : N (One doctor handles multiple appointments)
  • Patient Has MedicalHistory: 1 : N (One patient has a continuous timeline of health records)
  • Appointment Generates Billing: 1 : 1 / 1 : N (Every appointment produces an invoice record)
  • Doctor Prescribes Medication: M : N (Resolved via associative entity prescriptions)
  • Doctor Has Schedule: 1 : N (Doctor has weekly shifts across various days)
  • Doctor Belongs To Department: N : 1 (Doctors belong to a single department)
Normalization: The schema satisfies 3NF and BCNF by ensuring every non-key attribute is non-transitively dependent directly on the Primary Key.
